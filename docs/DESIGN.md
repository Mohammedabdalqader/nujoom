# Mobile design and state acceptance

Owner: Codex. Canonical app: `Desktop/nujoom`. This document specifies the first production identity slice and the real-versus-demo presentation. It is a handoff for Claude's auth/data implementation, not evidence that the screens or services already work. The owner's seven references and the existing floodlit visual language remain the visual baseline.

## Shared rules

- Arabic is the default, with full `dir="rtl"` and Arabic `lang`; English uses LTR. Mirror directional navigation, not football/action icons. Switch language without losing form progress.
- Keep the existing graphite, amber and emerald tokens and current type families. Use semantic success/warning/error tokens. Core form and body text is at least 16 px; interactive targets are at least 44 by 44 px. At 360 px and 200% text, labels wrap rather than clip or overlap.
- Preserve the five-tab player navigation. Reduce header density on small screens: city and account access take priority; theme can live in Settings. A notification dot or count appears only for unread real notifications.
- Every async surface needs initial, loading, empty, error, offline and success treatment. Loading must not briefly reveal demo records. An offline cache may be shown only with a visible last-updated time; availability and booking confirmation must never be presented as current from a cache.
- Never invent goals, ratings, achievements, friends, venues, matches or verification. Zero is a genuine count; absent or ineligible metrics render as unrated/unavailable. Email confirmation is not a football-skill verification badge.
- Do not use imagery of generated people as real players, nor generated pitches as real listed venues. Keep media rights and consent status attached to the data that renders each asset.

## Demo separation and media labels

- Demo is a separate build ID and data source with no production credentials. A persistent `نسخة تجريبية` / `Demo` marker is visible on every tab and detail screen, including captures. It cannot be dismissed and does not resemble a system badge or user verification.
- Generated people and pitches in demo carry `صورة توضيحية` / `Illustrative image` at the image or its immediate caption. Demo booking, score and social records are examples, not inventory or real user activity. A demo action cannot send real invites, mail, push, uploads or bookings.
- Production requires valid backend configuration and never falls back to fixture hooks. A fresh production account starts with real empty states. Build tests must check source selection and the bundle boundary, not only the presence/absence of a single string.

## Later pitch/map acceptance

The Jordan ambition is a coverage program, not a claim of complete national inventory. In-app search uses only Nujoom's prepared, reviewed catalog, not a live external search. Both statuses appear in the same list and geographic map: `غير موثق من نجوم الحارة` / `Not verified by Nujoom` and `موثق من نجوم الحارة` / `Verified by Nujoom`. The badge means operator participation, not merely a plausible location. A facility can contain several fields with different statuses, so results and badges are field-level.

Show each field's evidenced format, surface, indoor/outdoor, lighting, dimensions/layout and permitted real photos where known; use explicit unknown states otherwise. Keep source and last-review provenance accessible without making an imported record look owner-confirmed. Unverified details may offer directions if the coordinate is reliable and a correction/report action, but no Book, slots, guaranteed price, match creation or check-in. Only an owner-verified participating field with a real schedule gets those actions. Keep location permission optional; city search and an accessible list work without GPS or map tiles. An external directions link must not imply a reservation.

### G1 discovery mobile handoff (D-032; pending D1 contract review)

- **Entry and search.** Open on an accessible list for the user's chosen city, never a blank map or a live Google/OSM query. The city/name search and filters query only published Nujoom catalog fields. Keep `All`, `Verified by Nujoom` and `Not verified by Nujoom` as explicit badge filters; preserve search/filter state when switching list and map. Location permission is optional and requested only when the user selects Near me. A denied permission leaves city search fully usable. Do not say "all Jordan pitches" or imply a pin count is coverage.
- **Result card.** Use the physical field as the result unit. Show facility name, field label when needed to distinguish siblings, city/area, one text-and-icon verification badge, and only evidenced compact facts: players per side, surface, indoor/outdoor and lighting. Omit unknown compact facts; do not infer them from a photo or another field at the same facility. A missing licensed photo gets a neutral no-photo treatment, never a generated venue image. Price may appear only from confirmed operations. Book/slot actions render only when the server says that exact field is bookable; no client-side badge inference. A not-verified card has Details, not Book.
- **Map parity.** Map and list must expose the same published results and field-level badges; markers group a facility's sibling fields without merging their statuses. An unchecked location has no marker or Directions action and remains discoverable in the list with `الموقع غير مؤكد` / `Location not confirmed`. An approximate location has a visibly approximate marker and `موقع تقريبي` / `Approximate location`, but no turn-by-turn directions. Only map- or site-checked entrance coordinates may open directions. If tiles fail or data is limited, keep the list available and do not drop listings without coordinates from the result count.
- **Detail.** Lead with name, badge, access and location confidence; then show evidenced dimensions/layout, amenities and rights-cleared photos, with source/attribution and last-reviewed date. Distinguish an unknown amenity set from a reviewed empty set. Include Report a problem (wrong location, design details, closed, duplicate) and Add a missing pitch as moderated submissions, never immediate edits to the public catalog. Not-verified detail explains `هذا الملعب غير متاح للحجز عبر التطبيق بعد` / `This field is not bookable through the app yet` and offers no slots, match creation, check-in or implied operator partnership. Directions, when allowed, are not a reservation.
- **Availability states.** A verified partner can have zero current slots; say `لا توجد مواعيد متاحة حالياً` / `No times available right now` without showing a fake Book success. If the schedule integration is inactive, stale or unreachable, show bookings unavailable and withhold Book. The D1 contract must resolve whether the public verified badge persists during an inactive schedule; D-032 requires an active accurate schedule before a field first becomes verified. Never turn a cached slot into a current reservation.
- **Empty, offline and errors.** Distinguish (1) no reviewed catalog entries in this city, (2) catalog entries but no verified/bookable fields, (3) filters with no matches, and (4) offline/unavailable search. Each has a useful city/filter/retry action and truthful count. A cached directory may render only with a last-updated label; booking and report submission require server acknowledgement. Map failure falls back to the list without hiding the search.
- **Acceptance.** Check Arabic RTL and English LTR at 360/390 px and 200% text, light/dark, keyboard and screen reader. The spoken result includes field name, badge, location confidence and bookability; the badge is never conveyed by color alone. Every map pin has an equivalent list item and action; touch targets are at least 44 px. Verify one facility with two differently badged fields, unknown attributes, no coordinates, approximate coordinates, a closed/hidden field, and a verified field with no slots. G1 is a directory beta, not evidence of a working booking system.

## Recorded-match permission (S4/S5 acceptance)

- A durable recording opt-in never substitutes for a fresh, affirmative acknowledgement tied to the specific booking. Show who will record, what may be captured, the match time, and the visibility/retention rules before requesting it. No preselected yes. An unrecorded match remains available when permission is declined.
- Youth need their own match acknowledgement and a confirmed guardian approval tied to that recorded match, unless the owner explicitly approves a different scope after privacy review. A guardian's general account-level yes alone is not presented as consent for every future match.
- Start capture only after all required participant and guardian checks succeed on the server. Missing, expired, withdrawn or unreachable permission means no capture. A weak connection must not turn an unknown permission state into approval.
- Withdrawing during capture must stop the recording source promptly, not merely pause future clip triggers. The organizer sees a stopped state and cannot restart until the missing permissions are restored for this match. Already captured footage featuring the withdrawing person is held from sharing while deletion and moderation policy is applied; a participants-only downgrade is not sufficient by itself.
- The team must demonstrate how a remote withdrawal reaches a recording phone, including when either device loses connectivity. Until that behavior is verified, do not describe withdrawal as immediate or claim the recording flow is production-ready.
